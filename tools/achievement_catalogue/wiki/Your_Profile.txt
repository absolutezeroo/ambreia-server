[[Image:Achievbutton profile.png||Your Profile. Amount will be shown in green ribbon|thumb]][[Your Profile]] is a section of the [[achievements]] on [[Habbo]]. This section of achievements has 16 achievements with a combined total of 134 levels to unlock.

=== True Habbo ===
The True Habbo achievement usually takes a long time to complete, despite the player not having to actually do anything. It adds one point to each player's level for each day their account is registered on Habbo. This achievement does not depend on logging in, it will run even if the player goes away. In June 2012, the number of achievements awarded for online time increased from 10 to 20, adding more achievements for older users to unlock.

=== Online Time ===

To achieve this badge, a player must stay online in the hotel for X amount of minutes in order to receive the next level and badge. This achievement takes a while to complete; to reach level 20, a player must be online for x hours or x whole days. Once a player completes the minutes for one badge, it will take longer to reach the next level. In January 2013, the number of achievements awarded for the online time increased from 10 to 20, adding more achievements for older users to unlock.

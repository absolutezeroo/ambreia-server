[[Image:Achievbutton games.png|Achievement button, will display amount in the green ribbon|thumb]]The [[Games (Achievements)|Games]] section of [[Achievements]] is the largest collection on the hotel with a whopping 406 to earn. The [[achievements]] in this category range from tagging other players on a Skating Rink, completing jumps on a Skateboard and [[Crafting]] during campaigns. 

Until late 2012, there were only 4 different [[achievements]] to be earned. After the introduction of  the [[Habbo Games Hub]] more achievements became available, but after the feature was removed they became unavailable and are no longer listed under Achievements. Battle Banzai and Freeze achievements were previously listed in their own categories but have since been merged into the Games category.

[[Music (Achievements)]] was a previous category of [[achievements]] that was removed from the client with the introduction of [[Habbo 2020]]. These achievements related mostly to [[Jukebox|Jukeboxes]] and/or [[Trax Machines]]. With only two different achievements to earn in this section, it was notably the smallest of any sections. There were a total of just 30 different badges to earn in this category and just because there aren't many, it doesn't mean they won't be easy to earn. When this category was discontinued from the client, these achievements moved into the Archive.  

== Achievements ==

=== Music Player ===

The Music Player achievement was earned by playing a [[Juke Box]] in your room and setting it to play music. This achievement would only increase when there were other players in the user's room and they could hear the music. The progress for this achievement increased for each player that is in the room, so for example: There were 3 players in the room and they each stay for 5 minutes. This would equate to 15 minutes in total towards the achievement.

=== Music Collector ===

This achievement was earnt by purchasing different songs from the catalogue for use in a [[Juke Box]]. There were only 10 levels so it shouldn't be too expensive to reach the top, with the progress bar increasing each time a song was bought. Despite the hotel listing there only being 10 levels, the badge images have been uploaded to cover 20 levels.

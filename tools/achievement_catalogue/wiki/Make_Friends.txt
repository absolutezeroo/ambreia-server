[[Image:Achievbutton Mfriends.png|Make Friends. Amount will be shown in green ribbon|thumb]][[Make Friends]] is a section in the [[achievements]] tab on [[Habbo]]. Altogether in this section there are 115 achievements to earn for your Habbo. Only one of these badges relates directly to your friend count - the rest are about interacting with other users, such as respecting them or leaving sticky notes in someone else's room. As per usual, some will be much harder than others to achieve but that's where the fun lies!

== Achievements  ==

Popular methods to save credits include; asking a friend to trade the furniture back and sending gifts to other characters on the player's own ID.

=== Swamped By Stickies  ===

In this achievement, players must set up a room with a Stickie pole, so people are able to place stickies in their room. To complete this achievement a player must have a Stickie Pole and place it in a room. By inviting friends or creating an event, people will come into their room and drop stickies in their room. The more stickies people drop, the higher the achievement increases. This achievement was archived with the [[Habbo 2020]] update but unarchived in February 2024 with the Lunar New Year Challenge<ref name="multiple">https://www.habbo.com/community/article/33486/a-lunar-new-year-challenge</ref>

=== Nice As Pie ===

''This achievement is no longer available. '' To earn this achievement, all you had to do is give respect out to other players. Each day you are given 3 respects to give to whoever you like, whether it be a friend or a stranger. A common thing people do with respects is known as a 'respect swap', in that the two players each share their respects with eachother. Originally, there was only one badge for this but at some point in 2012, an additional 9 levels were added in. Once again in January 2013, a further 10 levels were added, bringing the grand total to 20 levels! The respect system was removed in the [[Habbo 2020]] update and was replaced with the [[Star Gems]] system and achievements.

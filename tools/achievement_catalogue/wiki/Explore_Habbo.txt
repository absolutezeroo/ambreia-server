[[File:Achievbutton ExploreHabbo.png|Explore Habbo. The number attained will be shown in the green ribbon|thumb]][[Explore Habbo]] is a section of the [[achievements]] available on [[Habbo]]. This is one of the biggest groups of achievements with the previous Flash client version of the hotel including a rather large 237 to collect, and the [[Habbo 2020]] client having 42 levels of achievements in this section. Some of the achievements that have previously been included under this section ranged from visiting Habbo rooms, growing and breeding monster plants, and going roller skating.

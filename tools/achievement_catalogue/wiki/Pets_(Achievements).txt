[[Image:Achievbutton Pets.png|Pets. Amount will be shown in green ribbon|thumb]]
There is a whole range of [[achievements]] to be earned from [[Pets]]. At present this figure stands at 260 achievements in total, ranging from how many pets you own, feeding pets and even as far as jumping obstacles on a horse. As with many [[achievements]], some will cost you [[credits]] while others may merely cost time.

== Achievements ==
=== Pet Owner ===
To earn this achievement, a player must simply own a certain number of [[Pets]]. The quantity required increases with each level up to level 10 with the possibility of more being added in the future. If you receive a pet as a gift, this will not count towards the tally. However, if you send a pet in a gift to someone else, this will count.

=== Pet Trainer ===

All pets can be taught special tricks, and this achievement can be earned by doing so. Different animals have different abilities - for example, a chicken can be trained to chicken dance, while a dragon can be taught to breathe fire. Each time your pet levels up, you will gain one point towards your next achievement level.

[[Image:Achievbutton BuildRoom.png|Build your Room. Amount will be shown in green ribbon|thumb]]
[[Build Your Room]] is one of the many sections within the [[Achievements]] section of [[Habbo]]. With a total of 150 achievements currently available in this section, it is one of the largest collections, ranging from achievements with only 5 levels all the way up to those with 20. Most of the achievements in this category will require the user to spend [[Credits]] and large amounts of Credits on some occasions. However, spending is not directly necessary 100% of the time as some achievements can be earned by having players score points in game rooms or simply having other users spend time in a room.

== Achievements ==

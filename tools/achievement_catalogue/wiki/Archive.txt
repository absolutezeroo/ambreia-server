[[Image:achievements_tab_archive.png||Amount of achievements unlocked will be shown in green ribbon|thumb]][[Archive]] is an achievement category on [[Habbo]]. Achievements in this section can no longer be obtained but still count towards level progress for players who obtained them in the past.

== Achievements  ==
=== VIP Member ===
In this achievement, all a player had to do was be a member of the [[VIP]] club for 48 months. To complete this achievement, a player had to buy VIP, and with each month the VIP Member achievement would go up by 1 in the levelling guide. If a player had VIP for 12 months, they would receive the level 2 VIP Member badge. These achievements are no longer available as VIP has been discontinued and rebranded back to [[Habbo Club]].

=== Habbo Citizen ===
''See: [[Habbo Helpers]] for more information on this achievement.'' 

The Trading Pass was brought into Habbo in February 2009 to make the trading environment a little safer. With this being brought in, it meant that new members could not trade straight away - meaning if a scammer or hacker made a new account to trade furniture, they would not be able to. The requirements to earn the trading pass meant that you must spend 1 hour in the hotel, be registered for 3 days, and have activated your email address. With the removal of trading in Habbo 2020, this achievement became unavailable, and Habbos who had already completed this achievement saw it added to the 'Archive' section of their achievements.

=== Five Words Of Wisdom ===

In this achievement, all a player must do is tag themselves 5 times. To complete this achievement, a player must go to their profile on the Habbo main page and click the "My Profile" widget. Under the player's Habbo image and motto, there is an option to add tags.

=== Master of Words (I) ===

In this achievement, all a player must do is edit their Habbo motto/mission once. A user could click the box below their avatar in the bottom right-hand corner to change their motto or they could previously (but not anymore) do it on the [[Me pages]]. Two different versions of this badge exist.
